QUICK STEPS (Windows 11, no MATLAB required)
-------------------------------------------

1) Pick your checker
   - Double-click start_dashboard.cmd for the CSS-styled browser dashboard (it will verify Windows/PowerShell first), OR
   - Double-click requirements_checker.cmd for the classic menu (now with bonus internet/disk/memory checks), OR
   - Right-click either .ps1 and choose "Run with PowerShell"

2) Scan everything
   - Dashboard: click "Scan all" (per-card buttons update instantly, and a Windows badge shows your edition/build)
   - Menu: press A then Enter (or run the bonus checks individually via options 5-7)
   - Missing items offer to open the official download page

3) Silent mode (optional)
   - Open PowerShell in this folder
   - Run: powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File requirements_checker.ps1 -ScanAll

4) Shareable zip (optional, from PowerShell)
   - Run: ./package_windows_checker.ps1
   - Send the generated requirements-checker-windows.zip
