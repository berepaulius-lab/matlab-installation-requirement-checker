Double-click guide (Windows 11, no MATLAB required)
--------------------------------------------------
1) Double-click "requirements_checker.cmd"
2) Press A then Enter to scan everything (or pick 1-4 for individual checks)
3) Read the emoji results:
   - ✅ item found
   - ⚠️ partially satisfied or older version
   - ❌ not found

Command-line alternative
------------------------
Open PowerShell here and run:

  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File requirements_checker.ps1 -ScanAll

Sharing a zip
-------------
Open PowerShell here and run:

  ./package_windows_checker.ps1

Send the generated requirements-checker-windows.zip to others so they can double-click and scan.
