START HERE (Windows 11, no MATLAB needed)
-----------------------------------------

1) Double-click **start_dashboard.cmd**
   - Click **Scan all**. Close the browser when you’re done.

   Prefer text only? Double-click **requirements_checker.cmd**, press **A + Enter**.

2) Let it fix things
   - Missing stuff? The app first tries a silent **winget** install.
   - If winget isn’t there, it offers the official download page.

3) Need help?
   - In the menu, press **L** to open the log folder or **B** to save a support ZIP you can share.

4) Share it
   - In PowerShell, run `./package_windows_checker.ps1` to build `requirements-checker-windows.zip`.
